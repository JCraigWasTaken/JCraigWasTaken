# John Craig

Engineering Manager and Software Architect at Moneris in Toronto. I have eight years of experience across fintech, cleantech and utilities, including five years leading software teams. I grew CarbonCure's software team from two people to twelve, then hired an eight-person team from scratch at Moneris. Most of my work is in TypeScript and Node.js on cloud backends, and I run a self-hosted LLM server at home on two AMD MI50 cards.
